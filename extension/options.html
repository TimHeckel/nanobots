mint a fine-grained token ↗
Each fine-grained token (Issues: write + Contents: read) is pinned to one user/org and its chosen repos — paste one per org and discover repos reads what each can reach, filling the list below and routing every call to the right token automatically. Classic tokens work too (capped to recent repos — prune below). Stored locally only.
Screenshots upload to your R2 bucket and the issue embeds the link — nothing is committed to git. Screenshot capture stays disabled until R2 is connected; reports file text-only in the meantime. Free: R2's free tier (10 GB, zero egress) is far more than screenshots need — Cloudflare may ask for a card to enable R2, but you won't be charged within the allowance.
